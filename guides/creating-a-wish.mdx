A **Wish** is an open-ended idea you want to do with your friends — "let's go hiking sometime" or "we should catch a show soon." Unlike a Plan, a Wish doesn't need a fixed date. Post one when you have a vague idea but aren't ready to commit to specifics yet. Friends can mark themselves as Interested, giving you a sense of who's keen before you decide to lock anything in.

## Creating a Wish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the create event modal">
    Tap the **+** button in your feed to open the event creation modal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Wish as the event type">
    Choose **Wish** from the event type selector at the top of the modal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the wish details">
    Complete the following fields:

    | Field | Details |
    |---|---|
    | **Title** | A short name for your wish (max 50 characters) |
    | **Description** | More context or details (max 200 characters) |
    | **Location** | A place, neighbourhood, or general area |
    | **Category** | The type of activity (e.g. Food, Outdoors, Music) |
    | **Timeframe** | A loose description of when, like "sometime next month" or "before summer" |
    | **Cover image** | An optional photo to make the card stand out in the feed |
    | **Visibility** | **Friends only** or **Friends of friends** |
    | **External link** | An optional URL for extra context (e.g. a venue website) |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Post your wish">
    Tap **Post**. Your wish appears in the feeds of everyone who matches your visibility setting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Posting

Once your wish is live, friends can tap the **Interested** button on the card. Every time someone marks themselves as Interested, the `interested_count` on your wish card increments — so you can see at a glance how much enthusiasm there is for the idea without anyone having to commit.

## Editing a Wish

You can update any field on a wish after it has been posted. Open the wish card, tap the three-dot menu, and select **Edit**. All fields — including the title, timeframe, location, category, and visibility — are editable.

## Copying a Wish

Use the **Copy Wish** option from the three-dot menu to duplicate an existing wish and start fresh from it. The copy is created as a new wish in draft state with all the same details, which you can then edit before posting.

<Tip>
  A wish with a high `interested_count` is a strong signal that your friends are on board. When the interest is there, convert it to a Plan to lock in a date and start filling spots.
</Tip>
