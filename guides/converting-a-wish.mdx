When a Wish has gathered enough interest and you're ready to commit, convert it into a Plan. Converting lets you add a date, time, and participant cap without losing any of the original wish details. It's the natural next step once you've gauged enthusiasm and want to make something real.

## Converting a Wish to a Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wish">
    Find the wish in your feed or on your profile and open the event card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the event menu">
    Tap the **three-dot menu** (⋯) in the top-right corner of the wish card.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Convert to Plan">
    Tap **Convert to Plan** (also labelled **Plan it** on your own wish cards) from the menu options.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the required plan fields">
    Fill in the fields that turn the wish into a concrete event:

    | Field | Details |
    |---|---|
    | **Date** | The date the event will take place |
    | **Time** | The start time of the event |
    | **Max participants** | The maximum number of spots (required, must be at least 2) |
    | **Min participants** | The minimum number of people needed (optional) |

    All other details — title, description, location, category, cover image, and visibility — carry over from the original wish.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the conversion">
    Tap **Confirm**. The event becomes a Plan and WishWe sends a notification to every friend who previously marked themselves as Interested, letting them know the event is now confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Changes After Conversion

When a wish converts to a plan:

- The event is now a **Plan** — it has a concrete date, time, and participant cap.
- The loose **timeframe** (e.g. "sometime next month") is removed, replaced by the specific date and time you provided.
- Every friend who marked themselves as Interested receives a notification that the event is confirmed and ready to join.
- The plan immediately inherits all capacity and expiry behaviour — it will fill up and expire like any other plan.

<Note>
  Converting a wish to a plan is **irreversible**. Once a wish becomes a plan, it cannot be changed back to a wish.
</Note>
