Your WishWe profile is the face other users see when they interact with your events or visit your page. The **Edit Profile** page brings all your personal details, avatar, and privacy controls into one place so you can keep everything current.

To open Edit Profile, tap your avatar in the sidebar and select **Edit Profile**, or navigate directly to your profile page and tap **Edit**.

## Updating Your Profile Info

The Edit Profile page exposes the following fields:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Username** | Your unique handle on WishWe — this appears on event cards and in search |
| **First name** | Your given name |
| **Last name** | Your family name |
| **Bio** | A short description of yourself (up to 150 characters) |
| **City** | Where you're based — helps friends find context for your events |
| **Date of birth** | Your birthday |
| **Gender** | Select Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say |
| **Social media link** | A URL to your social profile (e.g. `https://instagram.com/username`) |

Once you've made your changes, tap **Save changes** to apply them. The button is enabled only when you've modified at least one field.

## Uploading an Avatar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the avatar area">
    On the Edit Profile page, tap the pencil icon overlaid on your current avatar (or the placeholder avatar if you haven't uploaded one yet).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an image">
    Select an image from your device. WishWe accepts standard image formats.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crop the image">
    An in-browser cropper opens automatically. Zoom and drag the image to frame the part you want to use as your avatar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and save">
    Tap **Confirm** in the cropper, then tap **Save changes** on the Edit Profile page. Your avatar is uploaded and served through CloudFront CDN so it loads quickly everywhere on the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Privacy Setting

The **Privacy** section of the Edit Profile page contains a **Public profile** toggle. The toggle controls whether other users can see your profile and events:

- **Toggle on (Public)** — Friends and friends-of-friends can view your profile and events according to each event's visibility setting.
- **Toggle off (Private)** — Other users cannot see your profile page or any of your events, regardless of visibility settings.

Tap **Save changes** after adjusting the toggle for the change to take effect.

## Changing Your Password

To update your password, tap **Change password?** at the bottom of the Edit Profile form. A modal opens where you enter your current password followed by your new password. Submit the modal to save the new password — you don't need to tap **Save changes** on the main form separately.

<Tip>
  Your avatar and display name are visible to friends and friends-of-friends on event cards and in search results. Keep your avatar up to date so friends recognise you quickly when you join their plans.
</Tip>
