---
title: "Get Started With WishWe in 5 Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Create a WishWe account, set up your profile, add your first friend, and post your first wish or plan — all in under 5 minutes."
---

By the end of this guide you'll have a WishWe account, a complete profile, at least one friend, and your first Wish or Plan live on your feed — ready for your friends to see and respond to.

<Note>
  WishWe is live at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online). You can also download the app on **iOS** (App Store) or **Android** (Google Play) and follow these same steps from your phone.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) and choose how you want to sign up:

    - **Email** — enter your email address and click **Continue**. WishWe sends a 6-digit verification code to that address.
    - **Google** — click **Continue with Google** and authorise the connection. You're verified instantly and jump straight to onboarding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    If you signed up with an email address, check your inbox for a message from WishWe and enter the **6-digit code** on the verification screen.

    The code expires after a short window. If it doesn't arrive within a minute, check your spam folder or request a new code.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    After verification, WishWe walks you through a short onboarding flow. Fill in the following details:

    | Field | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | **Username** | Unique handle others use to find you. Choose carefully — this is shown to other users. |
    | **First name** | Displayed on your profile. |
    | **Last name** | Displayed on your profile. |
    | **Password** | Minimum 8 characters (email sign-ups only). |
    | **Bio** | A short line about yourself — optional but recommended. |
    | **City** | Helps friends find you and gives context to your events. |
    | **Date of birth** | Used for age-relevant features. |

    When you're happy with your details, tap **Finish** to land on your feed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first friend">
    You have two ways to connect with people:

    **Search by username**
    1. Tap the **Search** icon or navigate to **Find Friends**.
    2. Type a friend's username and tap their profile.
    3. Tap **Add Friend** to send a request. They'll get a notification to accept.

    **Share your invite link**
    1. Go to your profile and tap **Invite Friends**.
    2. Copy your personal invite link and send it via any messaging app.
    3. When someone signs up through your link, you're connected automatically — no request needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Post your first Wish or Plan">
    Tap the **+** button on your feed to create your first event.

    <Tip>
      Not sure about a date yet? Start with a **Wish**. You can capture the idea now, let friends express interest, and convert it into a Plan once you're ready to commit to a specific date and time.
    </Tip>

    **To post a Wish:**
    1. Select **Wish** from the event type options.
    2. Add a title, description, and a loose timeframe (e.g. "sometime next month").
    3. Set visibility to **Friends** or **Friends of friends**.
    4. Tap **Post**.

    **To post a Plan:**
    1. Select **Plan** from the event type options.
    2. Add a title, description, date, time, and a participant limit.
    3. Set visibility to **Friends** or **Friends of friends**.
    4. Tap **Post**.

    Your event appears on your feed and is visible to the audience you chose. Friends can join a Plan or mark themselves interested in a Wish right from their feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're all set! Head to your feed to see what your friends are planning, and keep an eye on your notifications for activity on your events.
