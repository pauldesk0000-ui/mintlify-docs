WishWe mobile brings the full web experience to iOS and Android. Built with React Native and Expo, the app shares the same backend and API as the web app at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) — so everything you do on one platform is instantly reflected on the other.

## Features

The mobile app gives you access to every feature available on the web:

- **Wishes** — post open-ended ideas with a loose timeframe and let friends mark themselves interested.
- **Plans** — create concrete events with a date, time, and participant limit; friends join until capacity is reached.
- **Friends** — send and accept friend requests, search for users, and share your personal invite link.
- **Notifications** — stay on top of friend requests, joins, interest reactions, event updates, and scheduled reminders.
- **Profile** — manage your username, bio, city, avatar, and privacy settings.
- **Sharing** — share deep links to any wish or plan with anyone, on or off the platform.

## Authentication

You sign in with your email address and password, or continue with Google OAuth. Your session is stored securely on-device — tokens are never held in JavaScript memory. Sessions refresh transparently in the background, so you stay signed in without interruption.

## Getting the App

The WishWe mobile app is built with [Expo](https://expo.dev) and runs on both iOS and Android. Follow the platform-specific guide to set up your environment, configure the backend connection, and launch the app.
