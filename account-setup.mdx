Your WishWe profile is how friends find you, recognise you, and decide whether to join your events. A complete profile — with a clear username, a photo, and a short bio — makes it easier for people to connect with you and trust the plans you post. This page explains every profile field, how privacy settings work, and how to manage your credentials.

## Profile Fields

<Note>
  Your **username** is set during onboarding and is the handle other users see when they search for you, view your profile, or receive notifications related to your activity. Pick something recognisable to your friends.
</Note>

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Username** | ✅ Yes | Unique across WishWe. Shown publicly to other users. |
| **First name** | ✅ Yes | Displayed alongside your username on your profile. |
| **Last name** | Optional | Displayed on your profile when provided. |
| **Bio** | Optional | A short description of yourself — shown on your public profile. |
| **City** | Optional | Provides location context for your events and helps friends find you. |
| **Date of birth** | Optional | Used for age-relevant features inside the app. |
| **Gender** | Optional | Shown on your profile if you choose to add it. |
| **Social media URL** | Optional | Add a link to an external profile (e.g. Instagram, X/Twitter). |
| **Avatar** | Optional | Your profile photo. WishWe includes an **in-browser cropping tool** so you can frame the image exactly how you want before uploading — no external editor needed. |

To update any of these fields, go to your profile and tap **Edit Profile**.

## Privacy Settings

WishWe gives you a **private profile** toggle (`is_private`) in your account settings.

- **Public profile (default)** — any WishWe user can view your profile, see your username and bio, and send you a friend request.
- **Private profile** — your profile details are hidden from users who aren't your friends. Only people you've accepted as friends can see your full profile and your event activity.

Regardless of your profile privacy setting, you can still control the audience for each individual event — every Wish and Plan is published to either **friends only** or **friends of friends** at the time you post it.

To toggle your privacy setting:

1. Go to your profile page.
2. Tap **Edit Profile**.
3. Toggle **Private account** on or off.
4. Save your changes.

## Changing Your Password

<Warning>
  You cannot change your email address after you sign up. Make sure you use an email address you have long-term access to when you create your account.
</Warning>

If you signed up with an email address and password (rather than Google), you can update your password at any time:

1. Go to your profile page and tap **Edit Profile**.
2. Scroll to the **Password** section.
3. Enter your **current password** to confirm your identity.
4. Enter your **new password** (minimum 8 characters).
5. Confirm the new password and tap **Save**.

If you've forgotten your current password, use the **Forgot password?** link on the login screen to reset it via email.

## Connected Accounts

WishWe supports **Sign in with Google** (Google OAuth), which lets you log in without a separate password.

- If you originally signed up with Google, your WishWe account is linked to your Google account. You sign in by tapping **Continue with Google** — no password required.
- If you signed up with an email address, your account is not linked to Google and you sign in with your email and password.

At this time, you cannot link or unlink a Google account after the initial sign-up. Choose your preferred sign-in method when you create your account.
