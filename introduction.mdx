WishWe is a social planning app for anyone who's ever said "we should do this sometime" and then watched it never happen. It gives you and your friends a shared space to capture loose ideas as **Wishes** and lock in real commitments as **Plans** — so the things you actually want to do together make it onto the calendar.

## What You Can Do

- **Post Wishes** — share an open-ended idea (like "let's go hiking sometime") with a loose timeframe. Friends mark themselves interested without having to commit to a specific date.
- **Create Plans** — set a concrete date, time, and participant cap. Friends join directly, and the plan fills up until it hits capacity.
- **Convert Wishes into Plans** — when a wish gains enough momentum, turn it into a confirmed plan in one step.
- **Connect with Friends** — send and accept friend requests, search by username, or share a personal invite link that automatically connects new sign-ups to you.
- **Control Visibility** — publish each event to **friends only** or **friends of friends**, so you decide exactly how far an idea travels.
- **Get Notified** — receive in-app notifications for friend requests, joins, interest, event updates, cancellations, and scheduled start reminders.
- **Share Events** — every event gets a shareable deep link you can send to anyone, even outside the app.
- **Use It Anywhere** — access WishWe from the web at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) or from the iOS and Android apps.

## How Events Work

Every idea in WishWe starts as either a **Wish** or a **Plan**, and the two are designed to work together:

1. **Wish** — You have an idea but no fixed date yet. You post it, friends express interest, and the idea stays alive instead of getting lost in a chat thread.
2. **Plan** — When you're ready to commit, you create a Plan with a real date, time, and capacity. Friends join until spots are filled, and the event happens.
3. **Wish → Plan** — If a Wish gains momentum, you can convert it directly into a Plan. Everything carries over, and interested friends are notified automatically.

Plans expire once the event date passes and can be archived when they're done, keeping your feed clean and focused on what's coming up next.

## Platforms

WishWe is available on:

- **Web** — [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online), accessible from any browser with no installation required.
- **iOS** — download the WishWe app from the App Store.
- **Android** — download the WishWe app from Google Play.

All platforms share the same account, so your wishes, plans, and friends are always in sync.
