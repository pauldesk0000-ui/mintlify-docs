Your friend network is the foundation of WishWe. The people you connect with determine whose Wishes and Plans you see in your feed, and — if you post with Friends of Friends visibility — which corners of the network your own ideas can reach. The bigger and more accurate your network, the more relevant and lively your WishWe experience becomes. WishWe gives you three ways to grow that network: search, friend requests, and personal invite links.

---

## Finding Friends

If someone you know is already on WishWe, the quickest way to connect is to search for them by **username**. Use the search feature to look up their username and open their profile. From there you can send them a friend request directly.

---

## Friend Requests

Connecting on WishWe is a two-step, mutual process: one person sends a request, and the other accepts or declines it. Until the request is accepted, the connection isn't active and neither person's events appear in the other's feed.

**Sending a request**

Open someone's profile and tap **Add Friend**. Your request lands in their notification feed as a friend request notification, and the friendship is created with a `pending` status.

**Receiving a request**

When someone sends you a request, you'll see it in your notifications. You can **Accept** the request — which moves the friendship to `accepted` status and connects you both — or **Decline** it to remove the pending request with no further action.

**After acceptance**

Once accepted, you each appear in the other's friend list, and your events become visible to each other according to your individual event visibility settings.

---

## Invite Links

Not everyone you want to connect with is already on WishWe. Your **personal invite link** solves that. When someone signs up for WishWe using your invite link, the platform automatically creates a friendship between you — no separate friend request needed.

Each invite link is unique to your account. You can find your invite link in your profile settings and share it anywhere: a group chat, a text message, social media, wherever feels right.

<Note>
  The same invite link works for everyone you send it to — you don't need to generate a new one for each person. Anyone who signs up through your link is automatically added as a friend.
</Note>

---

## Unfriending

If you ever want to remove a connection, you can unfriend someone from their profile page. Once unfriended, the friendship record is removed. Neither of you will see the other's events in your feed anymore (unless an event is visible through a mutual friend under Friends of Friends visibility). You can always send a new friend request in the future if you want to reconnect.
