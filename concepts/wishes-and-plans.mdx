Every idea starts somewhere — sometimes it's a firm date on the calendar, and sometimes it's just a vague "we should really do this." WishWe gives you two distinct event types to match exactly where an idea is in its life: **Wishes** for the open-ended, hopeful stage and **Plans** for when you're ready to commit. Knowing which one to reach for makes it easier to turn a passing thought into a real shared experience.

---

## Wishes

A Wish captures an idea before it has a date. Instead of pinning down a specific day and time, you describe the idea in broad strokes and set a **timeframe** — a freeform phrase like "sometime this summer" or "before the end of the year." This low-commitment format lets you put the idea out there without anyone feeling pressured to block off their calendar.

When your friends see a Wish, they don't join it — they mark themselves as **Interested**. That interest is a signal, not a commitment: it tells you (and everyone else) that enthusiasm exists, and it helps build the momentum needed to turn a vague idea into something real.

Key characteristics of a Wish:

- **No fixed date** — described by a freeform timeframe instead of a specific date and time
- **Interest, not commitment** — friends tap Interested rather than joining
- **Converts to a Plan** — once you have enough interest, you can promote the Wish into a full Plan
- **Copyable** — you can copy any existing Wish to start a fresh one with the same details
- **Status** — a Wish is either `active` (open for interest) or `closed` (no longer accepting interest)

---

## Plans

A Plan is a fully concrete event. You commit to a date, a time, and a maximum number of participants up front — WishWe uses those details to track capacity and manage the event's full lifecycle.

When your friends see a Plan, they **Join** it. WishWe tracks every join, decrements the available spots in real time, and prevents new joins once the plan is full. Once the event date passes, the plan expires automatically. When everything is done, you can **archive** the plan by marking it as completed.

Key characteristics of a Plan:

- **Date and time required** — must have a specific date and time
- **Capacity-managed** — requires a maximum number of participants; WishWe tracks the current participant count and remaining available spots
- **Joining** — friends join the plan (not just express interest)
- **Auto-expiry** — the plan expires automatically once the event date and time pass
- **Archivable** — you can mark a plan as completed once it's done
- **Status** — a Plan can be `active`, `closed`, or `completed`

<Note>
  A Wish can never be marked as `completed` — only `closed`. The `completed` status is exclusively for Plans. If you want to wrap up a Wish without converting it, close it instead.
</Note>

## Wish → Plan Lifecycle

When a Wish starts attracting interest and you're ready to commit to a real date, you can convert it into a Plan in a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build interest on your Wish">
    Share your Wish with friends and wait for them to mark themselves as Interested. Use the interest count as a gauge for whether there's enough enthusiasm to move forward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger the conversion">
    Open your Wish and choose **Convert to Plan**. WishWe keeps the title, description, location, cover image, and category from the original Wish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the Plan details">
    Provide the fields a Plan requires: a specific date, a time, and a maximum number of participants. You can also add an optional external link (for tickets, a map, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once you confirm, the event becomes a full Plan. People who were interested in the Wish will receive an `event_planned` notification, letting them know it's time to actually join.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Wishes vs. Plans at a Glance

| Feature | Wish | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Date required** | No — uses a freeform timeframe | Yes — specific date and time required |
| **Capacity** | No limit tracked | Maximum participants required; spots tracked in real time |
| **Friend action** | Mark as **Interested** | **Join** the event |
| **Status options** | `active`, `closed` | `active`, `closed`, `completed` |
| **Can be converted** | Yes → into a Plan | N/A |
| **Can be copied** | Yes → starts a new Wish | No |
| **Auto-expires** | No | Yes, after the event date passes |
