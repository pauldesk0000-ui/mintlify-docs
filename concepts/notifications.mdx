WishWe keeps you in the loop through an in-app notification feed that covers everything happening in your social planning life — from friend requests to last-minute event updates. Every time something relevant occurs, a new notification appears at the top of your feed, and the notification bell in the navigation bar shows a running count of anything you haven't read yet.

---

## Notification Types

WishWe generates notifications for the following events:

| Type | When you receive it |
|---|---|
| `friend_request` | Someone sent you a friend request |
| `friend_request_accepted` | Someone accepted your outgoing friend request |
| `joined_event` | A friend joined a Plan you created |
| `interested_event` | A friend marked interest in a Wish you created |
| `event_planned` | A Wish you were interested in has been converted into a Plan |
| `event_start_reminder` | A scheduled reminder sent before a Plan you've joined is about to start |
| `event_updated` | An event you joined or expressed interest in has been updated by its creator |
| `event_cancelled` | An event you joined or expressed interest in has been cancelled |

<Tip>
  Check your notification feed after accepting a friend request — you'll see a confirmation notification there. It's a good moment to browse their profile and see what Wishes or Plans they've recently shared.
</Tip>

## Read and Unread State

Every notification starts as unread when it first arrives. The notification bell in the navigation bar displays the count of all unread notifications, so you always know at a glance whether anything needs your attention.

When you open the notification feed, you can mark individual notifications as read, which clears them from the unread count. Notifications are ordered newest-first so the most recent activity is always at the top.
