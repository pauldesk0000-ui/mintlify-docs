WishWe gives you fine-grained control over who can see what you're planning. Every event you create carries its own visibility setting, so you can keep one plan tightly within your close circle while letting another travel further through your network. On top of that, a separate profile privacy toggle controls whether other people can view your profile at all. The two settings work independently, giving you flexibility at every level.

---

## Event Visibility

When you create or edit a Wish or Plan, you choose one of two visibility levels. This setting determines which users see the event in their feed.

### Friends Only

With Friends Only visibility, your event is shown exclusively to people who are already your direct friends on WishWe. Nobody outside your immediate friend list — not even friends of your friends — can see it in their feed.

Use this when the event is personal and you want a tight, trusted guest list.

### Friends of Friends

*(Default)*

With Friends of Friends visibility, your event is visible to your direct friends **and** to their friends — even people you haven't personally connected with yet. When a friend-of-a-friend sees your event, WishWe surfaces which mutual friend you share, so the connection feels natural.

This is the default visibility level for every new event you create.

<Tip>
  Use Friends of Friends visibility to help a plan discover new participants through your network. If you're organizing something where more people means more fun — a group hike, a sports game, a dinner — casting a wider net through your extended network is an easy way to fill spots.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Visibility applies per-event. You can mix and match across different wishes and plans — for example, keep a birthday dinner on Friends Only while setting a weekend hike to Friends of Friends. There is no single global setting that controls all of your events at once.
</Note>

## Profile Privacy

Separate from event visibility, your **profile** has its own privacy switch.

When your profile is set to **Private**, people who are not your friends cannot see your profile details or browse your events from your profile page. This is useful if you want to participate in events your friends share with you directly, without making your own activity broadly discoverable.

When your profile is **Public** (the default), anyone on WishWe who navigates to your profile can view it — your events are then visible according to each event's individual visibility setting.

| Setting | Who can see your profile |
|---|---|
| **Public** (default) | Anyone on WishWe |
| **Private** | Profile details and events are hidden from non-friends |
