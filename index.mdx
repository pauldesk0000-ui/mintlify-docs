WishWe bridges the gap between a vague idea and a confirmed plan. Post a **Wish** when you want to gauge interest from friends, then promote it to a **Plan** once people are on board. Whether you use the web app at [wishwe.online](https://wishwe.online) or the mobile app on iOS and Android, everything stays in sync so no one misses out.

## How WishWe Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Create a free account with your email address or continue with Google. Verify your email and set up your profile username in the short onboarding flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Friends">
    Search for people by username or share your personal invite link so friends can join and connect with you automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post a Wish or Plan">
    Have a vague idea? Post a **Wish** with a loose timeframe and let friends mark themselves as interested. Ready to commit? Post a **Plan** with a date, time, and capacity — friends can join until spots fill up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make It Happen">
    Once a Wish gains momentum, convert it into a Plan. Share the event link with anyone, track who's joining, and get reminders before it starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>
